STUDIO PHILOSOPHY // 08

About Studio.

Codden was founded on a simple realization: the digital agency landscape is painfully divided between creative design studios that cannot write production code, and IT outsourcers that lack all design taste and product judgment. We built Codden to merge both under one roof.

The Codden Standard

We are not a bloated 500-person agency with junior developers learning on your budget. We operate as an elite, high-touch engineering atelier where every member of our team is a senior contributor with proven production credentials.

When you build with Codden, you speak directly with the engineers authoring your architecture and the designers crafting your interface.

Full Intellectual Property

We believe you should own your technology. 100% of repository code, Figma components, database schemas, and deployment credentials transfer directly to your company upon milestone completion.

No proprietary vendor lock-in. No hostage codebases. Total transparency.
